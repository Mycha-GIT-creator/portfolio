About

I like corners — I might be a spider. That instinct to lurk in the shadows, understand hidden structures, and find what others miss? It turns out that's pretty useful in cybersecurity.

I'm currently pursuing my studies at Agusan del Sur State University (ADSSU), building my foundation in information security, networking, and ethical hacking. I started my college journey at Cor Jesu College where I spent two years studying BS in Computer Science before transferring to ADSSU — each step sharpening my passion for tech.

My toolkit is growing. I work with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, Java, and C#, and I'm continuously exploring the tools and techniques that define the cybersecurity landscape. Every dark corner is a learning opportunity.

Outside of programming, I'm someone who finds joy in the quieter things. I enjoy reading — anything that makes me think differently. I like listening to music as a way to reset and recharge. I have a soft spot for anime and gaming, which honestly sharpened my problem-solving instincts more than I expected. I also enjoy just taking walks and observing the world — the same curiosity that draws me to cybersecurity shows up in everyday life too. 🕷️
